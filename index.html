Easy dashi, wherever you areA quick Japanese stock for your batter

Shortcut: Stir instant dashi powder into warm water, following the packet directions, then cool it before mixing the batter.

From scratch: Soak a small piece of kombu (dried kelp) in 300 ml / 1¼ cups water for 20 minutes. Warm gently until steaming, remove the kombu, then steep a small handful of bonito flakes for 2 minutes and strain. Let cool.

Can’t find dashi ingredients? Use low-sodium chicken or vegetable stock, or simply use water. The batter will still work.
